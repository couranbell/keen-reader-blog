About Keen Reader

Keen Reader is a personal book blog: honest reviews, slow essays, and opinions on reading and the book world, written by someone who reads on the couch, on the train, and definitely a little too late at night.

No sponsored reviews, no star-rating inflation — just notes from one reader trying to pay closer attention.
